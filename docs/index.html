Built for watching

▶️ Multiple player methodsIFrame API, Direct Embed, and Privacy Embed. Your selection stays saved on your device.
⏪ Full controls10-second rewind/forward, seeking, previous/next video, fullscreen, and landscape viewing.
📐 Three viewing sizesCompact, Standard, and Cinema modes let you choose how much space the player gets.
🧮 Calculator disguiseAn optional native calculator with advanced scientific functions and a protected unlock code.
🌙 Calculator themesLight mode by default with a compact dark-mode switch.
🔄 UpdatesBuilt-in update checking and GitHub release integration.
